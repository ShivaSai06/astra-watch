One place that collects, checks and tells you.

Follow a measurement from your machines to your phone.

1

Your laptop reports in

A small program on your MacBook sends its numbers to Astra Watch every 30 seconds.

2

The server checks itself

Astra Watch reads the server's own numbers and the state of its running apps directly.

3

Everything lands in one place

It keeps the measurements and watches for trouble, such as memory staying too high for a few minutes.

4

You find out

The problem appears on your private dashboard, and Telegram sends you a message.

5

You decide

Astra Watch explains what it found. If you want to stop something, you confirm first. It never does it on its own.

I built it because I was tired of hunting for the cause.

I develop on a MacBook and run my apps on a remote Linux server. Every time something got slow I opened one tool after another, logged in to the server and typed commands. I wanted one page that would just tell me what was going on.

Astra Watch is a personal, open-source project made for my own setup. If it is useful to you, take it, read it and change it.
